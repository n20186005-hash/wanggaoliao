## Quick pre-visit checklist

| Facility | Available | What to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main path and boardwalk | Yes | Slippery after rain; watch your footing at night |
| Accessible viewing area | Yes | Grass slopes and stairs are not all step-free |
| Seats and stair handrails | Yes | Seats may be occupied at peak times |
| Public toilets | Yes | Check accessible specifications and temporary maintenance on site |
| Low-level path lighting | Yes | Helps walking but does not brightly illuminate the entire park |
| Permanent shops and supplies | Not guaranteed | Bring water, food and essentials |

## Public toilets

There are public toilets, but cleanliness, supplies and maintenance vary. Families with babies or people with specific needs may prefer a city-centre stop before climbing and should bring tissues. We could not verify current official specifications for accessible toilet facilities, so please do not assume they meet individual requirements.

## Paths, slopes and rest areas

The main paths and boardwalk link viewing spaces and are the best place to get oriented. Stairs and grassy slopes offer other perspectives but may not suit all visitors. There are seats, handrails, lawns and open areas; keep picnic mats clear of main paths, planted areas and slope edges. See the [accessibility and seniors' guide](/en/accessibility/) for wheelchairs and strollers.

## Lighting

Improvement works installed low-level and path lights to balance safe walking with the night-view atmosphere. Not every step or grassy corner is illuminated. Carry a small torch and slow down off the main route. Keep children beside you and away from roadways or steep edges.

## Food and supplies

Vendors are not a permanent facility. Bring water, light snacks, a jacket and a rubbish bag; take all waste away. For dinner or late-night food, consult [nearby food options](/en/nearby-food/).

## Ways to plan the visit

- **30–45-minute stop:** Use the main path, accessible viewing area and available seating.
- **Sunset into night:** Visit the separate viewpoint first, then the park; see the [comparison](/en/park-vs-viewpoint/).
- **Family or senior visit:** Arrive while it is light, locate toilets and the return route, then decide how long to stay.
