## Explore Jukuiju

Jukuiju combines the courtyard arrangement of a traditional Taiwanese *sanheyuan* residence with a two-storey, Baroque-inspired facade. Its symmetry, red-brick arcades, ornamental motifs and courtyard proportions show how local homes absorbed architectural fashions from different eras. It makes a compelling daytime cultural stop before Wanggaoliao's evening panorama, but check admission and reservations first.

### Visiting tips

- **Check reservations:** Official tourism information has advised advance booking for weekday visits. Open areas and sessions can change; do not assume unrestricted daily access.
- **Allow 45–75 minutes**, depending on the tour, exhibition and accessible rooms.
- **Visit in daylight** to see the facade and courtyard shadows, then head to Wanggaoliao after dark.

### Highlights

1. **Hybrid architecture:** A traditional three-sided courtyard layout meets a Western-style two-storey frontage.
2. **Ornament:** Study the arches, facade reliefs and red-brick details.
3. **Adaptive reuse:** Restoration gives a historic private residence a contemporary cultural role.

## About Jukuiju and its management

Built during the Japanese colonial period in Wuri District, Jukuiju is associated with local family history and the meeting of Chinese and Western styles. **It remains privately owned and is a Taichung City-designated historic monument.** The owner manages its reuse and visiting sessions under cultural-heritage protections; municipal heritage authorities oversee the preservation system. Municipal restoration involvement does not imply public ownership. Chen Shaozong reportedly built it around 1930–1940, incorporating arches, parapets, red brick and washed-stone ornament. Follow the operator's entry rules and consult the [Taichung Cultural Heritage Office record](https://www.tchac.taichung.gov.tw/building?pid=37&uid=33).

## Opening hours and tickets

This is not a round-the-clock public park. Opening dates, bookable slots and accessible spaces may shift with exhibitions, markets, repairs or operations. Consult the [official attraction information](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/attractions/intro/1500) before setting off, and never enter private courtyards without permission. General visits, guided tours, performances and workshops can have distinct prices and cancellation terms; no universal admission fee can be assumed.

## Getting there

**Driving:** Navigate directly to Jukuiju's designated visitor entrance, not Wanggaoliao. Allow time to park, walk and check in; do not stop in private access lanes.

**Public transport:** Plan via Wuri railway station or nearby connections, then verify current buses, walking distances and the last return service. If continuing to Wanggaoliao at night, arrange the return rather than extrapolating daytime frequency.

## Suggested visit sequence

1. **Confirm the booking:** Check the allowed time, entrance and visitor rules.
2. **Take in the building:** Compare the traditional courtyard arrangement with the two-storey Western-style facade from permitted areas.
3. **Examine details:** Look at the arcades, columns and bricks while following photography and tour restrictions.
4. **Continue the route:** Combine [Zhuifen Station](/en/attractions/zhuifen-station/) and Jukuiju, then travel to [Wanggaoliao Night View Park](/en/).

## History, architecture and photography

Jukuiju illustrates how a local Taiwanese household retained a traditional domestic plan while adopting fashionable Western decoration in the early twentieth century. Family life, craftsmanship and later conservation are all part of its history. Frame the symmetrical facade on its central axis, then explore arches, brickwork and upper-storey ornament. If photography is permitted, use the courtyard as foreground without leaning on walls or climbing architectural elements. Observe restrictions on tripods, portraits and interiors.

## Practical advice and accommodation

Do not make a visit here the only anchor of a full-day itinerary until the session is confirmed. Keep another nearby heritage stop as an alternative. Choose accommodation in Wuri or central Taichung according to the next day's transport, and consult the [Wanggaoliao directions](/en/directions/) before an evening visit.
