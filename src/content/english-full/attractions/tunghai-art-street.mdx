## Exploring Tunghai Art Street

The Tunghai Art Street shopping area developed along the hillside of **International Street in Longjing District**, near Tunghai University. It mixes distinctive small shops, cafés, craft products and residential lanes, with an artistic atmosphere and gently sloping streetscape. Unlike a night market, it can be explored during the day; individual businesses have different operating days and hours.

### Visitor tips and highlights

- **Time needed:** 60–120 minutes, depending on shop openings and dining plans.
- **Streetscape details:** Observe shopfronts, planting, signs and small corner spaces on both sides of the slope.
- **Nearby pairing:** First explore the campus around [Luce Memorial Chapel](/en/attractions/luce-chapel/), then walk along International Street. Continue to Wanggaoliao in the evening if transport permits.

## About Tunghai Art Street

Official sources also call the area “Ideal Country Art Street” (理想國藝術街). On International Street near Tunghai University, it brings together humanities, arts and community life. Shops and pedestrian spaces continue to change; old information about one business should not be presented as a fixed service for the entire district.

### Who manages the district?

**Management:** The local authorities responsible for public streets in Longjing and individual independent businesses. Art Street consists of public roads, community spaces and private shops; it is not managed by one ticketing authority. Road facilities follow local administration, while individual shops announce their own hours and events. [Taiwan Tourism Administration source](https://www.tad.gov.tw/m1.aspx?id=10149&sNo=0001112).

## Opening hours, admission and transport

There is no district-wide opening hour. If your visit depends on a particular café, craft shop or restaurant, check that business first. Walking through public streets is free; shopping and meals are charged by each shop. Navigate to **Tunghai Art Street / International Street**, not the Wanggaoliao viewpoint or Luce Chapel inside the university. For buses, look up stops along Taiwan Boulevard and in Longjing, then confirm the final walking distance.

## Discover its appeal: street architecture and culture

### Sloping streets

International Street forms a continuous streetscape across a hillside. Walk slowly to notice corners, small shop windows, exterior decoration and planting. Watch for level changes on slopes and under arcades.

### Creative shops

Pottery, accessories and cafés are among the types of business that give the area a different character from a conventional department store. Shops may change; this guide does not promise that businesses mentioned in older listings are still operating.

## Suggested walking route

1. **International Street entrance:** Arrive from a lawful drop-off or parking location.
2. **Small corner shops:** Follow public walking routes and browse shopfront design and product types.
3. **Café break:** Choose among businesses open that day without blocking residential entrances.
4. **Campus extension:** If time remains, visit [Luce Memorial Chapel](/en/attractions/luce-chapel/) to appreciate modern architecture.

## Visitor etiquette, photography and accommodation

Use viewpoints on public roads to capture the streetscape. Ask whether photography is allowed before entering shops, and never stop a car at a narrow corner for a photograph. Slopes can be slippery in the rain. For overnight stays, choose Longjing, Tunghai or central Taichung based on your next destination.

### Is Art Street the same as Tunghai Night Market?

No. Art Street is mainly along **International Street**, while the Tunghai Villa Night Market occupies different street sections. Navigate to each separately.

### Is there an admission fee?

There is no single ticket for the public streets. Shopping, dining or individual activities are charged according to the business concerned.
