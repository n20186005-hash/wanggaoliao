## Exploring Luce Memorial Chapel

Luce Memorial Chapel on the campus of Tunghai University is one of Taichung's most recognisable examples of modern architecture. Designed jointly by **I. M. Pei and Chen Chi-kwan**, its four upward-curving surfaces create a distinctive silhouette, while the ceramic tiles take on warm tones in daylight. The chapel serves religious and campus activities. Seeing its exterior should not be confused with having unrestricted access to the interior.

### Visitor tips and highlights

- **Time needed:** 45–90 minutes, including a walk around campus.
- **Photography:** Observe the roof curves and the proportions of the lawn from directly in front and from an angle of about 45 degrees.
- **Etiquette:** Follow university notices during worship, ceremonies or campus controls, and do not interfere with religious activities.

## About Luce Memorial Chapel

Rather than using a dense arrangement of traditional columns for its architectural effect, the chapel organises space through curved shell forms and light, bringing together modern structural design and religious symbolism. The surrounding grass also connects the building to Tunghai University's green campus as an important shared landscape.

### Who manages the chapel?

**Management:** [Tunghai University](https://www.thu.edu.tw/). Luce Chapel stands within the university campus; its facilities, activities, maintenance and visitor rules are managed by the university. Visitors should follow the latest campus notices.

## Opening hours, admission and transport

Walking around the outdoor campus, entering the chapel interior and attending worship **cannot all be described as open 24 hours**. Check the university's current visitor and parking rules before entering. For public transport, check stops along Taiwan Boulevard. Drivers must use Tunghai University's designated parking arrangements rather than parking anywhere on campus.

## Discover its appeal: history, architecture and culture

### Curved structure

The four curved surfaces suggest hands joined in prayer, with roof and walls forming a powerful unified shape. From a diagonal viewpoint you can follow the extension of each surface and the lines where they meet.

### Light and campus environment

Ceramic tiles, light near the roof apex and the lawn create changing colours throughout the day. The place is also part of student life and campus culture at Tunghai University, so respect its teaching environment.

## Suggested visitor route

1. **Campus entrance:** Confirm the university's designated visitor route for the day.
2. **View the chapel from a distance:** Begin on an open, permitted path or lawn to understand the building's proportions.
3. **Photograph from an angle:** Move to one side of the front and observe how daylight falls on the curves.
4. **Campus walk:** Continue only along allowed campus paths, then visit the [Tunghai Art Street district](/en/attractions/tunghai-art-street/) for food.

## Visitor etiquette, photography and accommodation

Do not enter closed spaces or photograph identifiable participants in worship and ceremonies without consent. Large-scale filming, commercial photography and drones are subject to university rules. Choose accommodation around Tunghai, Central Taiwan Science Park, Longjing or central Taichung according to your next day's plans.

### Can I enter the chapel?

Entry depends on university activities and the opening rules on the day. Do not assume the interior is accessible at any time.

### Can I combine it with Wanggaoliao?

Yes. Visit the campus architecture and Art Street during the day, then travel to Wanggaoliao in the evening. Plan the journey home in advance.
