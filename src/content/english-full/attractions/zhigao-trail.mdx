## Explore Zhigao Trail

Built beside the Wangtian irrigation canal, Zhigao Trail offers a relatively gentle, shaded route rather than a strenuous mountain ascent. The watercourse and vegetation make it a useful nature stop before a Wanggaoliao sunset, though the two places require separate transport.

### Visiting tips

- **Main route:** Allow about 60–90 minutes for walking and breaks; extend this for children, photography or a slower pace.
- **Extensions:** Xuelian Trail and Xuetian Mountain are separate branches requiring additional time, fitness and weather checks.
- **Equipment:** Wear grippy shoes and take water and sun protection. Rain can make paths slippery; cancel in heavy rain or under warnings.

### Highlights

1. **Wangtian irrigation canal:** Follow the channel around the edge of the Dadu Plateau.
2. **Family-friendly main route:** The official tourism description emphasises a gentle grade beside the canal.
3. **Improved entrance:** The city has upgraded greenery and resting space near the trailhead.

## About the trail and its management

Zhigao Trail lies around Wuri District and the Dadu Plateau, following Wangtian Canal. Its irrigation landscape provides a glimpse of local engineering and terrain as well as a pleasant walk. **Taichung City Government Tourism and Travel Bureau and other responsible authorities oversee different facilities.** The bureau improved approximately 945 square metres of green resting space near the entrance, but the canal, hillside branches and access area may have different managers. Observe closure and maintenance notices rather than assuming one agency manages the entire route. Read the [city's project explanation](https://www.tourism.taichung.gov.tw/3220321/post).

## Opening hours and costs

This outdoor trail has no museum-style ticket schedule. Heavy rain, unstable slopes, construction or canal maintenance may close sections. Walk during daylight, finish before dark and heed trailhead signs. The main public route has no fixed paid entrance; transport, food, equipment and optional guided services are separate costs.

## Getting there

**Driving and parking:** Navigate to the Zhigao trailhead, not Wanggaoliao Night View Park. Parking and road access can change. Do not block farm tracks, irrigation-maintenance roads or residents' entrances.

**Public transport:** Check current Taichung bus routes, nearest stop and walking distance. For an evening trip to Wanggaoliao, verify last departures and transfers; do not assume a direct shuttle exists.

## Route and branch decisions

1. **Prepare at the entrance:** Confirm direction, refill water and check shoes near the canal.
2. **Walk the main route:** Observe water flow, channel engineering and vegetation; share space with cyclists and walkers.
3. **Return or extend:** Turn back after the main stretch unless daylight and energy justify Xuelian Trail or other extensions.
4. **Continue to the night view:** Eat and rest before travelling to [Wanggaoliao](/en/); do not rush unfamiliar slopes in darkness.

## Human landscape, ecology and terrain

Wangtian Canal is an irrigation landscape. Its bends and embankments show how waterways were adapted to local terrain; stay behind rails and out of the water. The main path is relatively level, but hillside branches can be significantly harder. Wet leaves, mud and standing water after rain alter the difficulty.

## Photography, family visits and accommodation

Use the receding canal, shaded trees and winding path as daytime compositions, without standing on embankment edges. With children or older companions, a main-route out-and-back is sensible; do not add unknown branches simply because a photo looks appealing. Stay in Wuri or central Taichung according to the next day's plans, and connect to our [sunset guide](/en/sunset/) if continuing to Wanggaoliao.

## Safety, visitor impressions and navigation

The key choice is whether to walk just the gentle main route or tackle longer branches. Online impressions of mud and difficulty vary by season and cannot replace closure notices. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Zhigao Trail part of Wanggaoliao?

No. One is a canal-side trail and the other a night-view park; plan separate transport.

### Must I walk all the way to Xuetian Mountain?

No. An out-and-back on the main route is a complete visit, particularly with children or older travellers.

### Has the entrance been improved?

The city reported a [new landscaped resting area](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/event/newsdetail/10096). Actual availability should be checked on arrival.
