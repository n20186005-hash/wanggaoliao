## Exploring Zhukeng–Nanliao Hiking Trail

The Zhukeng–Nanliao Hiking Trail crosses the slopes of the Dadu Plateau in Longjing District. The municipal tourism route describes **three sections, A, B and C, totalling around 5.5 kilometres**. Timber boardwalks and stone steps alternate, and elevated points may look toward the Dadu River estuary and Taichung Port. The difficulty, walking time and access conditions of different sections should not be collapsed into a single supposed trail.

### Visitor tips and highlights

- **Time needed:** Around 90–150 minutes for an out-and-back visit on a shorter section. Walking all sections requires more time.
- **What to observe:** Timber decking, rocky and earthen slopes, woodland shade and elevated viewing platforms.
- **Before leaving:** Check the entrance route map, weather, construction notices and return direction. Do not assume that everyone can comfortably walk the total published distance.

## About Zhukeng–Nanliao Hiking Trail

The trail connects settlements on the Longjing Plateau with the surrounding hills and is used for local walks and hiking. Its value lies in woodland, terrain and connections with the Ruijing trail network—not an ancient city wall or architectural remains.

### Who manages the trail?

**Management:** Taichung City Government Tourism and Travel Bureau and the authorities responsible for the trail. The city has promoted connections between paths and facility improvements. Construction, repair and opening status of each branch follow current notices and on-site management. [Official project reference](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/event/newsdetail/8922).

## Opening hours, admission and transport

The outdoor trail is not staffed at all hours. It can be slippery after rain, and damaged sections must not be entered. Drivers should navigate to **Zhukeng–Nanliao Hiking Trail** and confirm the trailhead name. If arriving by bus and walking onward, check service times and the return journey. Do not assume that nearby Hunter Memorial Park and every trail entrance share one location.

### Parking and six distinct Google Maps pins

These are separate map listings, not interchangeable names for a single trailhead. Choose your intended A, B or C section and compare each pin with the approach road, on-site signs and current trail conditions before setting off.

1. **[Nanliao Community Parking Area](https://maps.app.goo.gl/xxbenL2cSScKtBCp9):** A parking reference when arriving by car and approaching the community on foot. Check actual parking availability and restrictions on site.
2. **[Nanliao Hiking Trail](https://maps.app.goo.gl/95NJDuBmq3wF5iep8):** A separate trail listing to cross-check against route boards.
3. **[Zhukeng–Nanliao Historic Hiking Path](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aXPTk8KSNPU5FBz48):** A listing using the historic-path name; pay attention to junctions, gradients and posted directions.
4. **[Zhukeng–Nanliao Trail — pin 1](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vXuLWVxZuEYgVMmD8):** The first of two independent listings with the same trail name.
5. **[Zhukeng–Nanliao Trail — pin 2](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CCHdWiRMmr27KRmg8):** A second listing with that same name; do not assume the two pins represent the same place.
6. **[Nanliao Trail](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vXFDq6vcDU7TUXh46):** Another trail marker to compare with your planned branch and return direction.

These six links help distinguish parking and trail references; they do not establish that every pin is directly accessible by car or that all sections are continuously open. Confirm a permitted pedestrian entrance from the parking area before following route signs.

## Discover its appeal: nature and trail engineering

### Hills and coastal-plain views

The terrain on the western side of the Dadu Plateau offers views toward the port and the flat land around the river mouth from certain elevated locations. Visibility varies with the weather and obstruction by trees.

### Trail infrastructure

Timber boardwalks and stone steps show how the route has been adapted to gradients and pedestrian safety. Stand securely when taking pictures; do not step beyond the path edge or trample vegetation for a better view.

## Numbered hiking route

1. **Parking and trailhead:** Drivers can first check the [Nanliao Community Parking Area](https://maps.app.goo.gl/xxbenL2cSScKtBCp9), then use on-site signs to confirm their A, B or C section and return time.
2. **Woodland section:** Follow clear signposts and watch for junctions.
3. **Viewing area:** If open, stop on a safe platform to look toward the estuary and coastline.
4. **Return or authorised connection:** Follow signs back. With enough time, you may continue toward the [Ruijing Hiking Trail](/en/attractions/ruijing-trail/), but this requires advance planning.

## Visitor etiquette, photography and accommodation

Bring shoes with good grip and sufficient drinking water. Protect yourself from summer sun, avoid dangerous slopes after rain and descend before dark. Suitable photographic subjects include woodland shade, the perspective of steps and distant views from safe platforms. Stay in Longjing, Dadu or central Taichung according to the next day's itinerary.

### Is this one single route?

No. The official description contains different sections A, B and C. Confirm your intended branch at the entrance.

### Can it be combined with the Ruijing Trail?

Some trail-network connections have been documented, but whether a through-hike is suitable depends on branch closures, fitness and transport for the return.
