## Exploring the Great Wall Hiking Trail

The Great Wall Hiking Trail in Dadu District takes its name from the winding course across the hills. Walkers can observe the shaded paths, slopes and views toward the coast from the Dadu Plateau. Here, “Great Wall” is a **descriptive nickname for the trail**, not the remains of an ancient Chinese defensive wall. The city tourism authority has previously described a one-way walk of around an hour, but actual time depends on fitness and which sections are open that day.

### Visitor tips and highlights

- **Suggested stay:** About 60–120 minutes, adjusted to currently accessible sections.
- **Hiking highlights:** Alternating steps, inclined paths and tree shade. Wear shoes with good grip and carry drinking water.
- **Important warning:** Official notices have previously described unstable slopes or damaged trail sections. Check the latest closure and repair notices before going; never cross a barrier.

## About the Great Wall Hiking Trail

This is a local leisure route in the Dadu hills. Its main interest is not a building but the way the winding path, rest areas and hillside outlooks follow the land. Updated mural walls depicting local history have also featured near the trail entrance.

### Who manages the trail?

**Management:** Taichung City Government Tourism and Travel Bureau and the authorities responsible for the relevant trail sections. The city bureau has previously improved the trailhead environment and painted walls. Trail repairs and access restrictions follow the latest on-site notices; old online guides must not be taken as proof that the entire route is open. [Official improvement reference](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/event/newsdetail/9006).

## Opening hours, tickets and transport

Although tourism information sometimes lists hiking trails as open around the clock, **damaged or under-repair sections must not be entered**. Before leaving, check the [municipal trail advisory](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/event/newsdetail/10008) and entrance signs. There is no general attraction ticket. Drivers should park legally; bus users need to confirm the walking route from their stop to the trailhead.

## Discover its appeal: nature, terrain and local culture

### Hilly landscape

The path follows changes in elevation, with occasional views toward the coast in clear weather. Tree canopy, slopes and open sections create contrasting scenery. Mud, fallen leaves and wet stone steps after rain can increase the risk of slipping.

### Images of local history

The painted landscape at the trail entrance has incorporated historical themes such as the Kingdom of Dadu, Ruijing's old wells and Huangxi Academy. This makes it possible to connect a hike with nearby cultural sites.

## Suggested hiking and sightseeing route

1. **Trailhead:** Check access announcements, the route map and the day's weather.
2. **Safe trail sections:** Follow waymarks and observe the tree shade and hillside contours.
3. **Return by the same route:** Turn around immediately if you encounter a closure, unstable surface or rain. Never cross control tape.
4. **Cultural extension:** Choose [Ruijing Village](/en/attractions/ruijing-village/) or [Huangxi Academy](/en/attractions/huangxi-academy/) as your next visit.

## Visitor etiquette, photography and accommodation

Take photographs from stable, sufficiently wide and legally accessible rest platforms, rather than standing at the outer edge of slopes. Afternoon sun can bring out the hill contours, but do not try unfamiliar side routes after dark. Accommodation in Dadu, Longjing or central Taichung can be chosen according to the next day's transport needs.

### Is this the same as the Great Wall trail in Fengyuan?

No. This guide refers to the trail in **Dadu District, Taichung City**. Include “Dadu” in your search to avoid navigating to Fengyuan.

### Is it suitable for families with children?

The official tourism authority has previously presented it as a family-friendly hiking route. Nevertheless, assess suitability based on each child's stamina, the weather and the sections currently open.
