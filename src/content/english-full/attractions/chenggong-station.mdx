## Explore Chenggong Station

Chenggong Station in Wuri District, Taichung, is an interesting stop for exploring local railways and the cultural significance of auspicious station names. Paired with the name **Zhuifen**, “Zhuifen–Chenggong” suggests the blessing of “pursuing success.” The station, however, remains an operating railway facility, and sightseeing or photography must not interfere with train operations or station services.

### Visitor advice

- **Suggested time:** 20–45 minutes without a train journey; if taking a train, plan around the day's timetable.
- **What to observe:** Photograph station signage, the entrance and exterior in daylight while watching for pedestrians and scooters.
- **Tickets:** Commemorative tickets and specified route tickets are not guaranteed to be sold in the same way at all times. Confirm arrangements with [Taiwan Railways](https://www.railway.gov.tw/).

### What makes the station special?

1. **A name associated with good wishes:** “Zhuifen–Chenggong” holds a particular place in popular railway culture.
2. **Railway travel:** Pair the stop with Zhuifen Station and take an actual train journey.
3. **Local history:** The station is connected to transport development in Wuri and local memories of the nearby Chenggongling area.

## About Chenggong Station

Chenggong is a station operated by Taiwan Railways in Wuri District. It attracts visitors because of the meaning of its name and the famous pairing with Zhuifen Station. It also performs an everyday public transport role, so passenger circulation, ticketing and platform safety come first.

### Who manages Chenggong Station?

**Operator: State-owned Taiwan Railway Corporation.** Chenggong Station is more than a photographic symbol of “pursuing success”: it remains part of Wuri's public transport network. Its name story meets the everyday activity of railway travel, with additional local associations to Chenggongling. Station buildings, platforms, service hours and commemorative tickets are subject to Taiwan Railways rules; the unrestricted access assumed at some tourist attractions does not apply to an active station. ## Discover Chenggong's culture

### Railway history and station names

The auspicious phrase “Zhuifen–Chenggong” has gradually become a popular railway souvenir theme among students preparing for examinations and other travellers. The story is worth exploring, but it does not mean railway services or commemorative ticket availability remain unchanged year-round.

### Architecture and photography

The station sign, entrance and train-shaped roof decoration are among the most recognisable visual details. Avoid close-up photographs of passengers without consent. Never enter tracks, back-of-house railway areas or restricted platforms without permission.

## Visitor etiquette, accommodation and FAQ

An active station is not an all-hours photography studio. Follow railway signs and rules. If combining the stop with Wanggaoliao's night view, arrange the return journey in advance. Choose accommodation in Wuri, around THSR Taichung Station or in the city according to the following day's itinerary.

### Is Chenggong Station the same as Zhuifen Station?

No. They are separate stations and can be visited on a railway-heritage outing on the same day.

### Is a “Zhuifen–Chenggong” commemorative ticket always available?

No guarantee can be made. Ticket availability, sales services and train schedules follow Taiwan Railways notices. Check before leaving.

### Can I walk directly from Chenggong Station to Wanggaoliao?

Do not treat them as neighbouring walking attractions. Plan the separate journey according to actual roads and transport options.
