## Exploring Dadu Wanxing Temple

Wanxing Temple on Ronghua Street in Dadu District is also known locally as the **Top Street Mazu Temple** (頂街媽祖廟). It is an important place for residents' everyday worship and festival activities. Visit at a respectful pace to understand the beliefs of the Dadu community, rather than simply taking a photograph of the entrance and leaving.

### Visitor tips and highlights

- **Time needed:** 30–60 minutes. Follow the temple's circulation arrangements during ceremonies or busy periods.
- **What to observe:** The front façade, roof-ridge decoration and the relationship between the temple forecourt and local worship space.
- **Nearby pairing:** Combine a daytime visit with [Huangxi Academy](/en/attractions/huangxi-academy/) to explore education and faith, then head to Wanggaoliao for the evening view.

## About Dadu Wanxing Temple

Wanxing's significance lies in its continuing role in local religious life. Mazu worship is connected with community activities across Dadu. The temple is not just an architectural relic: it carries residents' festivals and shared memories across generations.

### Who manages the temple?

**Management:** Dadu Wanxing Temple's own managing organisation. This remains an active place of worship. Worship, ceremonies, use of the grounds and building access follow the temple's notices. The Taichung City Government Tourism and Travel Bureau publishes public tourism information. [Official tourism reference](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/attractions/intro/1835).

## Opening hours, ticket prices and transport

Wanxing Temple is not a public park open at all hours. To enter the main hall, follow the opening times and ritual arrangements shown on site. Ordinary worship generally does not require an attraction ticket; offerings and related services are subject to personal choice and temple rules. Drivers should navigate to **Dadu Wanxing Temple, No. 154 Ronghua Street**, follow local parking signs and avoid occupying the forecourt or residents' entrances.

## Discover its appeal: history, architecture and culture

### Local Mazu worship

The name “Top Street” reflects the relationship between the temple and Dadu's older settlement. Its importance encompasses not just worship of the deity but also activities passed down within the community over generations.

### Architectural details

Observe traditional religious architecture through the roof ridge, doorway, columns and forecourt. If a ceremony is taking place inside, stop using flash and keep quiet.

## Suggested visitor route

1. **Neighbourhood approach:** Walk toward the forecourt while watching nearby traffic.
2. **Exterior:** Examine the front elevation and roof decoration from lawful public space.
3. **Respectful entry:** Follow temple guidance and do not interrupt worshippers.
4. **Cultural extension:** Visit [Huangxi Academy](/en/attractions/huangxi-academy/) to compare local educational and religious spaces.

## Visitor etiquette, photo viewpoints and accommodation

The safe area of the forecourt is a good place to photograph the building's front. Do not take close-up photographs of worshippers without consent or block people bringing offerings. If continuing to Wanggaoliao for the night view, select accommodation in Dadu, Wuri or central Taichung according to the next day's transport.

### Is Dadu Wanxing Temple the same as Nantun Wanhe Temple?

No. **Dadu Wanxing Temple is at No. 154, Ronghua Street**; Nantun Wanhe Temple is a different temple. Do not confuse the destinations when navigating.

### Can I combine it with Wanggaoliao in one day?

Yes. Visit Dadu's cultural sites by daylight, then reach a designated viewing space at Wanggaoliao in the evening.
