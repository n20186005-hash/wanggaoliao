## Explore the Fazi River waterfront

The giant **TAICHUNG** letters are the most recognisable feature of the Fazi River waterfront in Nantun District. The stepped grassy bank, open skies and urban gateway setting invite a short daytime walk and landmark photo. Unlike Wanggaoliao's elevated viewpoint, this is a low-lying riverside public space. You can visit both in one day but must use their separate map locations.

### Visiting tips

- **Allow 30–60 minutes**, or less for one landmark photograph.
- **Daylight** makes the white lettering, steps and sky easier to photograph. Verify any evening lighting schedule and path access locally.
- **Watch river conditions:** Avoid banks and closed paths after rain or when water levels rise.

### Highlights

1. **TAICHUNG letters:** A memorable city-name landmark.
2. **Riverside perspective:** Observe the levee, urban transport and expansive sky together.
3. **Easy short stop:** Less demanding than a hillside trail before an evening at Wanggaoliao.

## About the promenade and its management

Fazi River Gateway Waterfront Promenade is a public riverside landscape in Nantun District, listed as a distinct attraction by Taichung Tourism. **Taichung City Government Water Resources Bureau maintains the landscaping, plants and surroundings of the TAICHUNG landmark; the river channel also comes under central river authorities, including the Third River Management Branch.** The city developed roughly 1.8 km of riverside landscape as an arrival gateway and has described recurring grass and planting maintenance. Flood protection, ecology and urban welcome all shape this site. Never treat the river channel as a swimming or free-access recreation area. See the [city maintenance explanation](https://www.taichung.gov.tw/1541322/post).

## Opening hours and admission

An outdoor promenade is not a guarantee that every segment is open around the clock. River levels, heavy rain, embankment work and vegetation maintenance may close sections. Steps, slopes and banks are easier to judge in daylight. Check the current lighting and access situation if planning night photos. General landmark viewing has no standard entry ticket; parking, transport and temporary events may incur their own charges.

## Getting there

**Driving:** Navigate directly to the Fazi River Gateway Waterfront Promenade, park legally nearby and walk to the letters. Do not navigate to Wanggaoliao, the middle of the river or the surface of a nearby bridge. Never stop on bridges or bends to photograph.

**Bus:** Verify suitable routes, stops and walking approaches through [Taichung City Bus information](https://citybus.taichung.gov.tw/). For a later Wanggaoliao visit, plan evening transfers and your return separately; consult our [public transport guide](/en/public-transport/).

## Suggested route

1. **Find the lawful entrance:** Check levee paths and signs before walking in.
2. **Photograph the letters:** Frame the full word with steps and sky from outside the staircase traffic flow.
3. **Walk the riverfront:** Stay on open paths behind barriers.
4. **Continue uphill:** Eat and rest, then arrive at [Wanggaoliao Night View Park](/en/) before sunset.

## Urban design, engineering and hydrology

The oversized city name turns an entry corridor into a recognisable public destination. Changes in height between steps and embankments offer different views of the river and surrounding city. As a working river corridor, the landscape changes with flow, rainfall and levee maintenance; do not cross into the channel or assume every grassy area is safe to enter.

## Photography, practical advice and accommodation

- **Frontal panorama:** Frame the complete TAICHUNG lettering against the steps and sky.
- **Side angle:** Use the letters' perspective and grass lines without entering restricted zones.
- **Day versus night:** White letters are straightforward in daytime; night lighting depends on actual operation.

For a half-day outing, combine [Rainbow Village](/en/attractions/rainbow-village/) with this waterfront before Wanggaoliao. Choose lodging in Nantun or Wuri based on the next day's journey, not only straight-line distance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where are the giant TAICHUNG letters?

At the Fazi River Gateway Waterfront Promenade, not on Wanggaoliao's hilltop viewing platform.

### Are the letters lit at night?

Official tourism material has mentioned evening lighting, but equipment and operating times can change. Verify at the site.

### Does it combine well with Wanggaoliao?

Yes. Visit the waterfront by day and reach Wanggaoliao in time for sunset and city lights.
