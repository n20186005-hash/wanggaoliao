## Explore Rainbow Village

Rainbow Village is more than one mural: painted figures, animals, characters and bright patterns run across walls, doorways, paths and the ground. The former residential lanes have become an intimate public-art setting. Its vivid daytime details contrast with Wanggaoliao's broad evening skyline, making the two worthwhile stops on the same day.

### Visiting tips

- **Allow 30–60 minutes**, with extra time for close-ups and group photographs.
- **Visit in daylight** for the clearest colours; do not block narrow passageways while composing photos.
- **Check before leaving:** Exhibition areas, open days and activities may change. See the [official attraction listing](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/attractions/intro/574).

### Highlights

1. **Folk art in living space:** Paintings cover the walls and paths of a former military dependents' village, not gallery canvases.
2. **Expressive designs:** Compare close-up brushwork with wider compositions across whole facades.
3. **Short cultural stop:** Fit it into the day without sacrificing sunset time at Wanggaoliao.

## About Rainbow Village and its management

Located near Chun'an Road in Nantun District, the site preserves traces of residential community life as well as a distinctive painted landscape. Artist Huang Yong-fu covered his former neighbourhood with people, animals and blessing messages, helping make it a well-known example of vernacular art. **Taichung City Government's Cultural Affairs Bureau manages and maintains the venue.** Respect residents, designated exhibition spaces and conservation works. See the [municipal venue information](https://buskers.taichung.gov.tw/index.php?id=90&inter=space&page=3) and [city notice](https://www.taichung.gov.tw/3326720/post).

## Opening hours and admission

Exhibition areas and outdoor lanes may operate differently. Special activities, restoration and restricted sections can change access on a given day. Prioritise a daytime visit, consult the [official listing](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/attractions/intro/574), and follow signs on arrival. Ordinary outdoor viewing is generally free, while workshops or guided events may charge separately; do not mistake event fees for a permanent village-wide ticket.

## Getting there

**Car and parking:** Navigate to Rainbow Village at Lane 56, Chun'an Road, rather than Wanggaoliao. Park legally nearby and walk in; allow extra time at weekends.

**Public transport:** Check current Taichung bus routes, nearby stops and the final walking approach. If continuing to Wanggaoliao after dusk, verify evening connections and the return journey in advance; see our [public transport guide](/en/public-transport/).

## Suggested route

Photograph Rainbow Village in daylight, eat or restock in Nantun, travel separately to [Wanggaoliao Night View Park](/en/) before sunset, and stay for the blue-hour scene described in the [night-view guide](/en/night-view/). These are separate sites, not two areas of a single walkable park; include travel and parking time.

## History, architecture and things to notice

Military dependents' villages began as residential and community environments. The paintings added a new layer of collective memory to the modest houses. Notice the low building heights, narrow lanes and how images flow from door and window surrounds onto the paving. Understanding the original settlement gives meaning beyond a photo stop. Do not touch, climb or lean against fragile painted surfaces.

## Photography spots

- **Whole wall:** Step back to include openings, wall patterns and painted ground together.
- **Character details:** Capture colours, brushstrokes and expressions without touching the artwork.
- **Group portraits:** Watch for other visitors and avoid monopolising narrow routes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can Rainbow Village and Wanggaoliao fit into one day?

Yes. Visit the village during daylight and Wanggaoliao around sunset, allowing separate time for food and transfers.

### Can I come at night?

The colours are easiest to see in daylight, and exhibition areas may have access restrictions. Check current notices before departure.
