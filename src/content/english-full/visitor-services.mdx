---
title: "Visitor Services at Wanggaoliao | Toilets, Food and Emergency Help"
---

## 1. Parking

Wanggaoliao Night View Park is a free public park with generally free designated parking near the entrance. These are mostly roadside-style spaces, not a large multistorey facility. Capacity depends on signs and is more limited during long holidays. The Zhongtai Road approach has bends and low nighttime lighting; check traffic before opening doors. Large coaches, campervans and long motorcycles may struggle with turning space. There is no summit underground or multistorey paid car park; paid facilities tend to be downhill.

## 2. Public toilets

Public toilets are available near the entrance, but current accessible specifications and operating arrangements should be confirmed on site; we lack recent official measurements. Cleaning, consumables and temporary closures vary. If access or cleanliness is essential, use a city toilet first and bring tissues. Do not assume there is a nearby petrol-station or convenience-store toilet along the park path.

## 3. Food and restaurants

There is no permanent restaurant or reliably operating vendor on the ridge. A practical sequence is **dinner downhill → city views → supper back in the city**. Chun She, Ling Tung, Zhongtai Road approaches and the city offer chain restaurants, casual meals, hotpot and cafes; find current businesses on Google Maps. Do not expect dedicated vegetarian or halal-certified options at the summit: arrange dietary needs in the city before departure.

## 4. Convenience stores and supplies

There is no summit convenience store or supermarket, and the park does not sell water, snacks or umbrellas. Convenience shops and grocers can be found downhill around Lingdong and connecting roads, subject to their actual late-night hours. Bring water and snacks plus a rubbish bag and carry waste back downhill.

## 5. Fuel and electric-vehicle charging

Do not count on a summit or mountainside petrol station; plan refuelling after descending toward Wuquan West Road, Zhonggang Road, Xiangshang Road or major roads. There is no assured summit EV charger. Public AC and DC chargers may exist around HSR connections, malls and municipal car parks downhill; consult your vehicle's app or a current charging network for connector type and live availability. Leave sufficient range for the return.

## 6. Accommodation

There is no hotel accommodation inside the park. Business hotels, boutique stays, hostels, homestays and motels are found downhill in Nantun or near Taichung HSR, depending on your onward itinerary. **Camping, cooking and overnight stays in the park are prohibited.** Use a registered campsite elsewhere if camping is the goal. Pet boarding businesses in the city may require advance weekend booking.

## 7. Drinking water

The park has no guaranteed public drinking fountain. Bring your own water, preferably not in glass bottles, and keep bottles off paths and handrails where someone might knock them over.

## 8. Mobile connectivity

The Dadu Plateau ridge often has usable mobile voice and data service, but dips, outer parking areas and viewing edges can have terrain-related dead spots. Make calls from safe main paths or seating areas rather than standing at a precipice. Public summit Wi-Fi is not widely available; download offline navigation beforehand.

## 9. Emergency help

- **110:** Police.
- **119:** Fire and ambulance.
- **112:** Mobile emergency number; availability can depend on device and network.

For the current responsible emergency hospital, consult Taichung City health authorities before visiting. Emergency vehicles approach via Zhongtai Road. If someone needs urgent help, call **119** and describe their position relative to the park entrance or main path; do not move a seriously injured person unnecessarily. Although the main ridge path is simple, leaving it at night is risky. Retrace your route to a safe marked area if disoriented.

## 10. Other reminders

- **Limited mobility:** The main walkway and an accessible viewing area exist, but stairs and slopes are not fully step-free; read the [accessibility guide](/en/accessibility/).
- **Smoking:** Follow the park's posted restrictions and local rules.
- **Pets:** Keep dogs leashed, clean up waste and consider other visitors after dark.
- **Lighting:** Low-level lights are not floodlighting. Bring a torch for stairs and grass.
