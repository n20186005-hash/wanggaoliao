## Full-day overview

Plan **around 12 hours, 09:00–21:00**, with Wanggaoliao as the highlight after late afternoon. This approach suits first-timers, families and small groups who prefer nearby stops rather than constant detours. In rain or when tired, replace Zhigao Trail with Old Nantun Street or an indoor city heritage visit.

## Morning, 09:00–12:00: low-altitude daylight

1. **Breakfast and preparation:** Eat in Nantun or near Taichung HSR; obtain drinks, snacks and rainwear.
2. **Rainbow Village (45–60 minutes):** A colourful daytime stop, with an indicative city approach via Xiangshang Road, Lingdong Road and Zhongtai Road. The original itinerary estimates **20–30 minutes' drive**, but check traffic and actual route. Avoid rain and confirm current access.
3. **Zhuifen Station (30 minutes):** Enjoy the wooden railway building and “Zhuifen–Chenggong” ticket story.
4. **Jukuiju (book in advance):** Study the Baroque-inspired brick facade and courtyard. Although a quick exterior stop might take 20 minutes, interior access is session-dependent.

## Lunch, 12:00–13:30

Eat around Chun She, Ling Tung or Ling Tung University. Find current restaurants on Google Maps; choose a real meal downhill and do not plan cooking at the summit.

## Afternoon, 13:30–16:30: canal and local culture

5. **Zhigao Trail (60–90 minutes):** Follow the relatively gentle canal-side main route and return before evening.
6. **Optional Chun She history:** Learn about Wangtian place-name traditions and oral accounts of farming from Qing to Japanese periods; add only if time and energy permit.
7. **Prepare for night:** Refill water and bring a low-brightness or dual-colour headlamp.

## Late afternoon, 16:30–18:30

8. **Arrive at Wanggaoliao** approximately 60 minutes before the day's actual sunset.
9. **Park safely and orient:** Find the main path and distinguish the viewpoint from the park.
10. **Find directions:** Sunset generally falls toward the west or northwest, with the Taichung cityscape to the north.

## Evening, 18:30–21:00

11. **Sunset → blue hour → night view:** The blue-hour phase may provide around **20–40 minutes** of attractive light; allow at least half an hour if conditions permit.
12. **Viewpoint versus park:** Return to established paths at night rather than lingering alone along an unprotected ridge edge.
13. **Photography and snacks:** Carry all rubbish away.
14. **Prepare to leave:** Drink water, check belongings and ensure lights and driving arrangements are ready.

## Late return and rest

Drive slowly with headlights on mountain bends and watch approaching vehicles. Check fuel or charge around Wuquan West Road or Zhonggang Road downhill. If fatigued, consider a hotel in Nantun or near HSR rather than an unsafe overnight drive.

## Avoid these mistakes

- Do not arrive from far away at 03:00 or force a 04:00 ridge trip when tired.
- Do not squeeze every attraction into one day: Zhigao and Jukuiju are optional depending on bookings.
- Do not spend the entire day relying only on convenience-store food when proper meals are available downhill.
- Do not bring open flames or cook in the park.

## Variations

- **Dry weather:** Follow the outdoor circuit.
- **Rain:** Substitute Old Nantun Street or a Taichung museum for Zhigao; reassess the evening weather before climbing.
- **Step-free priority:** Use the main path and accessible viewing area, avoiding stairs; see [accessibility](/en/accessibility/).
- **With children:** Follow the [family itinerary](/en/itineraries/family/) and favour Rainbow Village and the easy Zhigao section.
