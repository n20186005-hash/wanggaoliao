## Family itinerary overview

This low-effort, safety-first route suits families with **children aged 3–12**, older relatives or friends. **Budget around 12 hours**, leaving about **09:00** and returning around **21:00**. On rainy days or when energy is low, exchange outdoor painting stops for a city museum or another sheltered break.

## Morning, 09:00–12:00: easy daytime stops

1. **Breakfast and supplies:** Eat in Nantun or near Taichung HSR station; collect water, snacks and rain gear.
2. **Rainbow Village (45–60 minutes):** Colourful artwork is accessible to children; avoid wet conditions and check current opening areas.
3. **Zhuifen Station (20 minutes):** See the historic wooden station and the “Zhuifen–Chenggong” name story; keep clear of active tracks.
4. **Jukuiju (allow for a booked visit):** Its facade and courtyard appeal across generations. **Do not assume a 15-minute walk-in:** reservations and permitted access depend on the operator.

## Lunch, 12:00–13:30

Choose food around Chun She or Ling Tung, favouring comfortable, familiar meals for children and older people. Refill water downhill.

## Afternoon, 13:30–16:30: low exertion

5. **Zhigao Trail (30–45 minutes):** Walk only a short, gentle stretch near the canal entrance, rather than committing to the full trail with tired companions.
6. **Optional Chun She local-history walk:** Explore the oral traditions associated with “Wangtian Kan” and the locally named “Tunghai Fort,” while distinguishing folklore from verified heritage records.
7. **Prepare for the ridge:** Refill bottles, provide a low-brightness headlamp for children and windproof clothes for older visitors.

## Late afternoon, 16:30–18:30

8. **Reach Wanggaoliao:** Aim for roughly one hour before that day's sunset; seasonal sunset times vary.
9. **Park and orient:** Use lawful spaces and hold younger children's hands.
10. **Learn the compass:** Point out east, west, north and south as a simple family activity.

## Evening, 18:30–21:00

11. **Sunset, blue hour and city lights:** Stay only as long as everyone's energy permits.
12. **Night View Park:** Keep children on the main path; older visitors can use handrails.
13. **Photos and snacks:** Give older relatives somewhere to sit rather than standing for extended periods.
14. **Before leaving:** Ask children what they enjoyed and check everyone is ready for the return.

## Later return and rest

Use headlights and drive slowly on the winding road. Check fuel or charge downhill around Wuquan West Road or the city. If too tired to drive safely, rest overnight in Nantun or near the HSR station rather than forcing a late return.

## What to avoid

- Do not start a family ridge outing at **03:00–04:00** when everyone is exhausted.
- Keep children away from ridge edges even when supervised.
- With young children or older relatives, choose a short section of Zhigao rather than its entire route.
- Do not bring flames or cook in the park; follow fire restrictions.
- Never allow children off the main path alone.

## Alternatives

- **Dry day:** Follow the outlined outdoor route, subject to actual opening times.
- **Wet day:** Replace Rainbow Village with a city museum or sheltered Nantun stop; reconsider the ridge if visibility is poor.
- **Accessible group:** Concentrate on the main path and accessible viewing area, avoiding stairs; see [accessibility](/en/accessibility/).
- **Teenagers:** Consider more of Zhigao Trail and safe nighttime roadside-free photography, without entering prohibited zones.
