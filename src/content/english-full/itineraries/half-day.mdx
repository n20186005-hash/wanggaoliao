---
title: "Classic Taichung Half-Day Itinerary"
---

**Route overview: Rainbow Village → early dinner nearby → Wanggaoliao sunset → Night View Park. See several signature sights in southwest Taichung in a single half day.**

## Timeline (assuming sunset at 6:00 p.m.)

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 2:30 p.m. | Reach Rainbow Village for photographs and a leisurely stroll |
| 4:00 p.m. | Have a simple early dinner around Lingdong or Chunan Road |
| 5:10 p.m. | Drive uphill toward the lookout platform |
| 5:30–6:00 p.m. | Sunset at Wanggaoliao |
| 6:10 p.m. | Transfer to the Night View Park |
| 6:20–7:30 p.m. | Blue hour and the illuminated Taichung skyline |
| 7:45 p.m. | Descend; finish the trip or continue for a late-night snack |

## Transport connections

- The drive from Rainbow Village to Wanggaoliao generally takes around 10–15 minutes. Their proximity and convenient direction of travel are what make this route work.
- Driving or riding a scooter is easiest. Without a vehicle, compare direct services on bus routes 99 extended service or 323. If you plan to stay later, consider booking a ride back; see the [bus guide](/en/public-transport/).

## What to focus on at each stop

### Rainbow Village

Its painted surfaces look most colourful in daylight. The village gets crowded on weekends; arrive earlier if you want photographs without many people in the frame.

### Early dinner

Eat somewhere convenient around the Lingdong commercial district or Chunan Road. The priority is to reach the hilltop before sunset.

### Wanggaoliao sunset → night view

First watch the sunset in the direction of the lookout platform. After sunset, move to the Night View Park to enjoy the sea of city lights. These two viewpoints face different directions, so do not reverse the order. See the [two-viewpoint comparison](/en/park-vs-viewpoint/).

## Important notes

- The success of this itinerary depends heavily on **weather**. Check the [live camera](/en/live-camera/) for visibility before leaving.
- Allow more time for parking at both Rainbow Village and Wanggaoliao on weekends.
- Sunset is earlier in winter; start the whole itinerary roughly one hour earlier.
