---
title: "Date-Night Itinerary"
---

**Route overview: dinner in the city → Wanggaoliao night view → a late-night snack or dessert in Taichung. Allow around 4–5 hours in total. Driving or riding a scooter is the easiest way to follow this route.**

## Timeline (assuming sunset at 6:00 p.m.)

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 4:30 p.m. | Dinner on Gongyi Road or in Nantun |
| 5:40 p.m. | Leave for the hilltop; allow time to find parking |
| 6:10 p.m. | Arrive at Wanggaoliao, stroll and choose a viewing spot |
| 6:20–7:00 p.m. | Blue hour and the illuminated city skyline |
| 7:30 p.m. | Head back down the hill |
| 8:00 p.m. | Finish with dessert or a late-night snack in the city |

The sun sets earlier in winter, so move the entire schedule forward by roughly an hour. Check the sunset time for your actual date before leaving.

## Why this order works

- **Eat before going uphill:** There are no proper dining facilities on the hilltop. Being hungry can spoil an otherwise lovely night view.
- **Blue hour is the highlight:** About 20–40 minutes after sunset, the sky retains layers of colour. This is more romantic—and generally easier to photograph—than a completely dark sky.
- **Finish with a late-night treat:** You can usually return to the urban area in around 20–30 minutes after descending. Dessert or a snack gives the outing a natural ending.

## Small touches for a better date

- Bring a jacket for a partner who feels the cold. The wind is stronger on the hilltop, so that extra layer may be very welcome.
- Bring a picnic mat. Sitting side by side on the grassy slope to watch the city lights is more comfortable than standing throughout the visit.
- Take your couple photograph during blue hour. There is still blue in the sky and enough ambient light to show faces.
- Weekday evenings are usually quieter and more relaxed than weekends.

## Important notes

- On weekends, add about 20 minutes to your schedule for parking. Read the [parking guide](/en/parking/).
- Check the [live camera](/en/live-camera/) before leaving. If the hill is foggy, consider switching to a city-centre alternative.
- Drive slowly on the hill roads when descending late at night. A romantic outing should end safely.
