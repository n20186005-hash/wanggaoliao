title: "Taking the Bus to Wanggaoliao"
---

**You can take a bus directly to the stop named “Wanggaoliao.” Routes 99 Extended, 30 Extended, 323 and 355 currently serve the stop. What matters most is checking the direction of travel and the service available for your return journey.**

## Four direct routes: choose, check both directions, alight, follow the entrance signs

| Route | Main endpoints | Best starting areas | Selected stops | Official route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **99 Extended** | Jingwu Station ↔ Taichung Motor Vehicles Office | Central Taichung, Daqing MRT Station, THSR Taichung Station | Taichung Park, Daqing MRT, THSR Taichung, Rainbow Village, Wanggaoliao | [Real-time arrivals](https://citybus-free.taichung.gov.tw/driving-map?route=991) |
| **30 Extended** | First Square/Gancheng ↔ Taichung Motor Vehicles Office | Taichung Station area, Nantun and Lingdong | First Square, Nantun, Ling Tung University, Wanggaoliao | [Real-time arrivals](https://citybus-free.taichung.gov.tw/driving-map?route=3010) |
| **323** | Rainbow Village ↔ New Jianguo Market | Rainbow Village, Wuri and South District transfers | New Jianguo Market, Wuri/Lingdong area, Wanggaoliao, Rainbow Village | [Real-time arrivals](https://citybus-free.taichung.gov.tw/driving-map?route=323) |
| **355** | Renyou Parking Lot ↔ Xiyuan Senior High School | Xitun, Fuke Road and the Tunghai area | Xiyuan Senior High, Fuke Road, Taichung Motor Vehicles Office, Wanggaoliao | [Real-time arrivals](https://citybus-free.taichung.gov.tw/driving-map?route=355) |

**Do not check only the outbound journey.** These four routes differ in frequency, direction and final departure. Switch between both directions on the official page and make sure a return bus will still be available after your night-view visit. Open the [Taichung real-time bus information service](https://citybus.taichung.gov.tw/).

## Choose a route based on your starting point

- **Taichung Station or First Square:** First check routes 99 Extended or 30 Extended; both can directly reach the Wanggaoliao stop.
- **THSR Taichung Station or Daqing MRT Station:** Start with 99 Extended, which passes both stops.
- **Rainbow Village, Wuri or Lingdong:** Compare 99 Extended and 323. Choose according to the actual next arrival and the more reliable return service.
- **Xitun or Fengjia:** Check 355 first, then verify transfers and waiting times with Taichung's live bus data.

Routes may change with time of day or roadworks, so memorising a fixed departure time is not recommended. On the day of travel, use [Taichung's real-time bus service](https://citybus.taichung.gov.tw/) and check the outbound and return directions separately.

## Walking from the bus stop

1. Make sure the stop name is **Wanggaoliao**; do not get off too early at a similarly named stop.
2. Follow the park entrance signs and pedestrian route beside Zhongtai Road. Roadside lighting is dim at night, so switch on your phone light if necessary.
3. For the illuminated city panorama, head to **Wanggaoliao Night View Park**, not the separate Wanggaoliao Lookout Platform. See [the comparison](/en/park-vs-viewpoint/).

## Three workable plans without a car

- **Bus there and bus back after blue hour:** Good for travellers on a budget. Note your intended return departure before arriving.
- **Bus there, taxi or ride-hail back:** Suitable if you want more time for the night view. Check whether cars are available in the area before travelling uphill; do not leave this until after missing the last bus.
- **Share a car with two or more people:** Splitting the fare can reduce the uncertainty of transfers and waiting at night.

## What you must do for the return journey

- The outbound and inbound stops for the **same route may be on opposite sides of the road**. Confirm where you will wait for the return bus as soon as you arrive.
- Save screenshots of the route number, return direction and intended service. Do not rely solely on the very last departure from a hillside stop.
- If you want to stay for the full night view, have a backup such as a ride-hailing service or pickup by someone travelling with you. Waiting times may increase later in the evening.
- Confirm vehicle availability in your ride-hailing app before heading uphill.
- Consider arranging a return pickup time with a driver on the way there.
- While waiting, stay at the bus stop or a lit area. Do not walk along the bends of Zhongtai Road.

## Common mistakes

- Confusing a regular route with its **extended** variant and boarding a service that does not reach Wanggaoliao.
- Checking only the outbound journey without verifying the return direction and that day's timetable.
- Treating the Night View Park and Lookout Platform as the same destination.

If your schedule is tight, prioritise the combination with the most dependable return journey, not simply the quickest outbound trip. For car and taxi arrival information, see [How to Get to Wanggaoliao](/en/directions/).
