---
title: "Wanggaoliao Photography Guide"
---

**The best time for photographs is blue hour, about 20–40 minutes after sunset. Even a phone can capture a clean panorama of the city lights if you use night mode and find a steady support.**

## Choosing the time of day

- **Before sunset:** Go in the direction of the lookout platform for sunset and silhouettes.
- **Blue hour:** The sky is deep blue and the lights are already on. This is the most photogenic 20-minute window for night views.
- **After full nightfall:** A completely black sky suits photographs dominated by city lights and long-exposure light trails.

For the order of stops, consult the [half-day photography itinerary](/en/itineraries/photography/).

## Smartphone photography tips

- Turn on **night mode**. Tuck your elbows close to your body or rest the phone on a railing or solid stone surface.
- Tap to focus on lights at a middle distance, rather than the dark grass immediately in front of you.
- Lower exposure compensation slightly so bright city lights are less likely to blow out.
- For a group photo, use a **wide-angle lens and stand toward the front**. Keep people relatively close to the camera and the city lights in the background.
- Bring a small torch or use a companion's phone to cast a little fill light on faces.

## Camera photography tips

- **Wide-angle lens:** Captures the entire spread of lights and layers of sky colour; especially useful during blue hour.
- **Telephoto lens:** Compresses the skyscraper cluster of the Seventh Redevelopment Zone, creating a dense metropolitan feel.
- Starting settings to try: ISO 100–400, aperture f/5.6–f/8, shutter speed 2–15 seconds, increasing the exposure time as the sky moves from blue hour to darkness.
- **Tripod:** There is room on the grassy slope and near the steps, but keep the legs clear of main walkways and watch for people tripping on busy evenings.

## Recommended shooting positions

- **Upper grassy slope:** An open, wide panoramic composition.
- **Stepped area:** Foreground lines add depth and structure.
- **Either side of the paths:** Move away from the centre when crowded to keep people out of the frame.
- For sunset, go toward the separate lookout platform. See the [difference between the two viewpoints](/en/park-vs-viewpoint/).

## Before you leave

1. Check the [live camera](/en/live-camera/) for visibility. Fog can obscure the sea of city lights.
2. Look up the day's sunset time and plan backwards from blue hour.
3. Check your battery and memory-card capacity. There is nowhere convenient to recharge on the hilltop.

## Common mistakes

- Arriving when the sky is already completely black and missing blue hour.
- Setting a tripod in the middle of a path and blocking other visitors.
- Using heavy digital zoom on a phone to shoot distant scenes, destroying image quality. Shoot wider and crop later instead.
