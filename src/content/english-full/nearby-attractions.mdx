**For colourful photos, choose Rainbow Village; for a walk, choose Zhigao Trail; and for heritage buildings, combine Zhuifen Station with Jukuiju. Finish at Wanggaoliao for sunset and the night skyline.**

## Choose by available time and energy

- **One or two extra hours:** Choose Rainbow Village or the Fazi River waterfront before Wanggaoliao.
- **Walking with children:** Allow roughly an hour on Zhigao Trail's main canal-side route, then rest, eat and head to Wanggaoliao.
- **A cultural half-day:** Combine Zhuifen Station and Jukuiju, adding Zhigao Trail only if time permits.
- **Already dark:** Go directly to Wanggaoliao. Treat Dadu Blue Road as a possible approach, not a compulsory photo detour.

## Three logical combinations

### Easy photography circuit — about 3–4 hours

Rainbow Village → [Fazi River lettering](/en/attractions/fazi-riverside/) if time allows → food in Ling Tung or Nantun → sunset at Wanggaoliao Viewpoint → city lights at the night-view park. Ideal for first-time visitors and groups who prefer less walking. Reserve daylight for the painted village and evening for the ridge.

### Nature walk — about 4–5 hours

Zhigao Trail → refreshments or early dinner around Ling Tung → Wanggaoliao sunset and night view. The main path is gentle but still requires suitable shoes and breaks. If adding Xuelian Trail or Xuetian Mountain, reduce other stops.

### Railway and heritage circuit — about half a day

Zhuifen Station → [Chenggong Station](/en/attractions/chenggong-station/) → Jukuiju when bookings allow → dinner → Wanggaoliao. Confirm Jukuiju's available session before deciding the order of the stations and trails.

## Final checks before departure

- Allow extra driving time around sunset and on holidays.
- Reserve Jukuiju before leaving; check Taiwan Railways services if riding or buying station tickets.
- Zhigao's main route is approximately **1.5 km** and official guidance estimates around one hour; extensions need extra time.
- Deduct dinner, parking and a sunset-before-arrival margin before adding another sight.
