## Food on the ridge

There is no guaranteed convenience store or permanent restaurant inside the park, and occasional vendors are unpredictable. Plan meals before or after your visit and take only drinks or small snacks uphill.

## Dinner before going up

### Ling Tung commercial area — about 10 minutes by car

Around Ling Tung University in Nantun District, student-oriented snack stalls, lunchbox shops and drink vendors provide one of the nearest, quickest and relatively inexpensive resupply options.

- **Where:** Lingdong Road near Ling Tung University.
- **Style:** Casual student food for lunch, dinner and takeaway drinks.
- **Hours:** A pre-visit stop around **17:00–21:00** can be practical; some individual businesses stay open after 22:00, but verify each one.
- **Find places:** [Ling Tung food on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=嶺東商圈+餐飲).

### Downtown Nantun — approximately 15–25 minutes by car

- **Gongyi Road:** Hotpot, barbecue, Italian and Japanese options suited to a sit-down date dinner; some operate into late evening, but times vary.
- **Old Nantun Street:** Local snacks and traditional noodle shops.
- **Liming Road and Yongchun East Road:** Chain restaurants and affordable eateries.
- **Map searches:** [Gongyi Road restaurants](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=公益路+餐廳) and [Old Nantun Street snacks](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=南屯老街+小吃).

### Around Rainbow Village

For a half-day tour, eat around Rainbow Village or Chun'an Road before travelling uphill. [Search nearby food](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=彩虹眷村+餐飲).

## Late-night food after descending

- **Zhongxiao Road Night Market:** A long-established late-night area with fried food, noodles and grills; often busiest roughly **23:00–01:00**, subject to individual vendors. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=忠孝路夜市).
- **Fengjia Night Market:** Very wide choice, but also weekend crowds. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=逢甲夜市).
- **Gongyi Road:** Some barbecue and hotpot establishments serve later sit-down meals.
- **Desserts and drinks:** Many city branches close around 22:00–23:00; confirm current hours.

## Dinner, night view and supper combinations

After Wanggaoliao, a descent toward Nantun, Wuquan West Road or Gongyi Road connects onward with central Taichung:

1. **Gongyi Road dinner → night view → Zhongxiao Road Night Market supper.**
2. **Budget dinner around Ling Tung → night view → supper near Liming or Yongchun East Road.**
3. **Food around Rainbow Village → night view → takeaway drink downtown.**

Specific opening hours change frequently. Search Google Maps immediately before leaving rather than trusting a permanent restaurant list.

## Match the meal to your group

- **Couples:** Gongyi Road dinner, Wanggaoliao, then city desserts; see the [date-night route](/en/itineraries/date-night/).
- **Families:** Eat earlier in Nantun and descend after blue hour so children are not up too late.
- **Short on time:** Bring takeaway from Ling Tung and carry all rubbish back downhill.
- **Supper enthusiasts:** Head toward Zhongxiao Road or Fengjia after the night view.
