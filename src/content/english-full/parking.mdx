## Parking locations and the park entrance

The night-view park entrance and parking approach are along **Zhongtai Road**. Slow down for the last bends and follow signs rather than staring at navigation. Use only marked or explicitly permitted roadside spaces; never block access roads, entrances, turns, fire routes, lawns or private driveways. Spaces closest to the entrance fill first. If full, continue to a safe turning or exit point rather than waiting on a bend. **Wanggaoliao Viewpoint is not the same as the Night View Park parking entrance**; see the [two-site comparison](/en/park-vs-viewpoint/).

## Walking safely after dark

Lighting around parking and pathways is limited. Use your phone light or a torch, watch stairs, rails and grassy edges, and keep children and older visitors together. Boardwalks and grass can be slippery after rain. Save or photograph your parking position before leaving the car. Carry a jacket and light so you do not have to unpack repeatedly at a dark roadside.

## Cars versus scooters

### Cars

- **Cost:** The park's designated public roadside/open parking has generally been free; follow current signs.
- **Spaces:** Marked roadside bays near the entrance are the first to fill.
- **Timing:** Try arriving **60 minutes before sunset** to improve your chance of finding a lawful space and learn the main path.
- **Turning:** The approach has successive bends. Never stop in a curve waiting for a vacancy.

### Scooters

- **Cost:** Designated scooter spaces are generally free.
- **Where:** Use marked motorcycle spaces and follow posted guidance elsewhere.
- **Limits:** Smaller size is not permission to park on grass or obstruct pedestrians.
- **Road safety:** Use headlights and lower speed on steep, winding, wet or gravelly sections.

## Free and paid options

The park itself does not operate a separate commercial paid car park. **Free does not mean park anywhere.** Road shoulders, lawns, approaches, bends and emergency access must remain clear. If the ridge's lawful spaces are full, paid public or private parking may be available down in Nantun or Chun She, but it is not an adjacent overflow lot; consider leaving and returning off-peak instead.

## Weekends and sunset peaks

The hour around sunset is often the busiest. Arrive early, let pedestrians pass and avoid stopping in the entrance to discuss parking. A departing wave may follow full darkness; if safe, wait briefly before joining traffic.

## What to do when full

1. Drive onward to a safe place to turn or exit; never queue on a bend.
2. Use only spaces explicitly allowed by signs, not any open-looking private plot or shoulder.
3. If none are legal, leave and return at a quieter time or switch to an arranged ride.

## Rain, darkness and enforcement

Use headlights without dazzling oncoming drivers. Wet roads, boards and grass are slippery; fog reduces visibility. Consult the [live camera](/en/live-camera/). Bring warm clothing and light before getting out. Noise-monitoring and illegal-parking enforcement may apply on Zhongtai Road, as detailed in [directions](/en/directions/). Do not stop across double-yellow lines for pictures.

## Leave the car downhill instead

City-to-ridge taxis have previously been estimated around **NT$250–350**, but actual fares vary; organise the return first. Bus route numbers sometimes cited include **99 extension, 30 extension, 323 and 355**, but schedules and last departures must be checked for your date in the [public transport guide](/en/public-transport/).

Before leaving, verify the exact destination, fuel or battery range, saved return route and that valuables are not visible inside the car.
