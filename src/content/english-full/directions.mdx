## Set your navigation

Search Google Maps for **Wanggaoliao Night View Park** or use this page's navigation button. Look for Zhongtai Road in Nantun District, not a similarly named place. For the nearby sunset platform, search **Wanggaoliao Viewpoint** separately; see [how the two sites differ](/en/park-vs-viewpoint/).

## Routes from common starting points

Driving estimates below are for ordinary conditions. Allow extra time during holidays and the sunset rush, and check live traffic before leaving.

### Taichung Railway Station and the old city

- **Car or scooter:** Follow Fuxing Road, Wuquan South Road and Xiangshang Road toward Dadu Mountain, then Zhongtai Road. Approximately **30–40 minutes**.
- **Public transport:** Transfer toward Taichung HSR station or a city transport hub and connect to a bus, or use a taxi. A bus-only journey can be circuitous; see [public transport](/en/public-transport/).

### Taichung HSR Station and Wuri

- **Car or scooter:** Head toward Ling Tung University and climb the hill, approximately **15–25 minutes**; Zhigao Trail is another possible stop in this area.
- **Public transport:** Connect from HSR to an applicable service for the Wanggaoliao stop and walk the final stretch. Check last departures carefully in the [bus guide](/en/public-transport/).

### Fengjia

- **Car or scooter:** Via Fuke Road or Taiwan Boulevard toward Nantun, then Wuquan West Road and Zhongtai Road, approximately **25–35 minutes**.
- **Public transport:** No straightforward direct connection; transfer via the city or Nantun, or take a taxi. Consider a [date-night](/en/itineraries/date-night/) or [half-day](/en/itineraries/half-day/) outing.

### Nantun and the Seventh Redevelopment Zone

- **Car or scooter:** Take Wuquan West Road or Xiangshang Road toward Dadu Mountain and Zhongtai Road, approximately **20–30 minutes**.
- **Public transport:** Check routes **99 extension, 30 extension, 323 and 355** for currently applicable Wanggaoliao services rather than assuming all run on your date; see [bus information](/en/public-transport/).

### Scooter

From the city, allow roughly **30–40 minutes**. Use headlights and lower speeds on bends, slopes, loose gravel or wet pavement. Never stop casually on bends or shoulders.

### Taxi or ride-hailing

The outward trip from the city is generally easier to arrange than a late-night pickup on the ridge. Confirm available cars or agree on a return time in advance. Driving yourself may be simpler if the [parking conditions](/en/parking/) suit your group.

## Mountain-road safety

Slow down for successive bends and oncoming vehicles. Lighting is limited at night: stay with established roads rather than shortcuts. In rain or fog, consult the [live camera](/en/live-camera/) before leaving.

## Speed limits, vehicle-noise cameras and enforcement

Zhongtai Road and nearby access roads may feature noise monitoring, speed enforcement and traffic cameras. **Observe posted signs rather than relying on a fixed speed:** some sections are described as **40–50 km/h**, with lower limits possible around bends and settlements.

- **Noise cameras:** Deliberate engine revving, loud exhausts and modified vehicles may trigger enforcement, particularly in the evening.
- **Solid double-yellow lines:** Do not cross or make prohibited U-turns for a better photo.
- **No roadside photo stops:** Bends, road shoulders and grass slopes are unsafe and may be subject to parking enforcement.
- **Modified motorcycles and cars:** Excessive exhaust noise can lead to automated detection or roadside inspection.

Check your vehicle, exhaust noise and current road signs before travelling. The aim is a safe visit without a traffic penalty.

## Common mistakes

1. Selecting the wrong “Wanggaoliao” result instead of the night-view park.
2. Leaving no time for holiday traffic and parking, then missing sunset.
3. Going up without a car or confirmed return and finding no late-night ride. Read the [bus guide](/en/public-transport/) first.
